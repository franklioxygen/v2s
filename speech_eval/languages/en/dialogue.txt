A: Hey, are you still coming to the market with me this afternoon?
B: I want to, but my sister just called and asked me to help her move a sofa.
A: Oh no, the big green one she bought last year?
B: Exactly that one. It barely fits through the door.
A: Then let's go to the market tomorrow morning instead.
B: That works for me. What do you need to buy anyway?
A: Mostly vegetables, some fresh bread, and a small gift for my neighbor.
B: Is it her birthday again already?
A: No, she watered my plants while I was away on vacation.
B: That's really kind of her. Maybe you could bring her some flowers.
A: Good idea. I think she loves sunflowers.
B: The flower stall near the old bridge usually has the nicest ones.
A: Great, let's meet at the bridge then.
B: Sounds good. I'll send you a message when I leave the house.
A: Perfect. Good luck with the sofa!
B: Thanks, I'm definitely going to need it.
