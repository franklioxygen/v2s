The city council approved a new plan on Monday to expand public transport across the northern districts.
Under the proposal, several bus routes will run more frequently during the morning and evening rush hours.
Officials said the changes aim to reduce traffic congestion and cut air pollution near schools.
Construction of a new tram line is expected to begin early next year, pending final environmental approval.
Local business owners welcomed the decision, saying better connections could bring more customers to the area.
However, some residents raised concerns about noise and the loss of parking spaces along the main avenue.
The council promised to hold public meetings before any construction starts.
